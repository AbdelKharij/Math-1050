\begin{abstract}
    Interactive homework and exams on the mathematics of social choice: voting theory and
    fairness criteria, weighted voting and measures of power, fair division,
    apportionment, and game theory. Each semester's materials form their own section, and
    a final section collects exams from earlier years.
\end{abstract}
